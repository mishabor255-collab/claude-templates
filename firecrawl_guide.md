# PICK — compare products from links with Firecrawl

Firecrawl reads the product pages you supply to Claude.
Try it with printers, monitors, or household appliances.

## Connect without a terminal
1. Open the [Firecrawl guide for Claude](https://docs.firecrawl.dev/quickstarts/claude-code).
2. Under Add to Claude, click the connection button.
3. Sign in to Firecrawl and review the requested permissions.
4. Check your Firecrawl credits and plan; cloud usage has limits.
5. Start a fresh Claude chat and enable the connected Firecrawl tool.

## Try a printer comparison
6. Paste two or three specific product page URLs.
7. Ask: “Use Firecrawl to read only these pages. Compare printing technology, duplex printing, connectivity, and compatible cartridges. Link each fact to its source. Mark missing information as unavailable.”
8. For running costs, also provide cartridge pages showing prices and rated yields.
9. Ask Claude to separate manufacturer claims from calculated costs per page.
10. Check the original listings before buying anything.

## If a page fails
Login requirements or site restrictions can prevent extraction; ask Claude to identify gaps.
Do not request a bypass. You can manually provide specifications you can access.
