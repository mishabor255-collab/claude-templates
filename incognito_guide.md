# GIFT — have a temporary conversation in Claude

Incognito chat keeps a conversation out of chat history and Claude’s memory.
Try brainstorming a surprise for a friend.

## In the desktop or web app
1. Start a new chat outside a project.
2. Click the ghost icon in the upper right.
3. Check for the Incognito chat label.
4. Ask: “Help brainstorm a surprise at home for a friend. First ask about their interests. Don’t buy or send anything.”
5. Discuss the options and choose an idea.
6. Copy any useful details into a separate note before closing.
7. Close the temporary conversation with the x in the upper right.

## Keep in mind
You cannot reopen a closed incognito chat or convert it to a regular one.
Incognito chats are available outside projects.
Being absent from history does not mean instant deletion by the service.
Retention is normally 30 days; organizational policies may require longer retention.
Team and Enterprise chats may appear in organizational exports.
In the new Claude experience, incognito chats cannot create files or run code.
Keep printed surprise clues out of sight. The ghost cannot hide your wall.
