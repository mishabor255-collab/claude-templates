# VOICE — rehearse an interview with Claude

Voice mode lets you speak to Claude and hear its replies.
Use it to practice answering interview questions aloud.

## Start on desktop or the web
1. Open a new Claude chat on your computer.
2. Click the sound-wave icon at the bottom right of the chat.
3. Allow microphone access if your browser or app asks.
4. Say the practice prompt below and wait for a spoken response.

## Practice prompt
“Run a mock interview in English for an internship. Ask one question at a time. Wait for my answer, then give brief feedback and suggest a more natural version.”
5. Answer aloud, then try the improved response in your own words.
6. Ask for slower speech or simpler questions when needed.
7. Request a recap of your strengths and phrases to practice.
8. Click Stop to leave voice mode.

## Check your setup
If you only get dictated text, check that you chose the waveform, not the microphone.
Start somewhere quiet; Claude’s feedback can be imperfect.
Voice conversations use your plan’s regular usage allowance.
This is practice, not a hiring guarantee or a language certificate.
