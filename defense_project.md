# DEFENSE — rehearse your thesis defense in a Claude Project

A Claude Project keeps your files and a standing instruction for every chat inside it.

## Steps (Claude app or website)
1. On the left: **Projects → Create project**, name it "Thesis defense".
2. Upload to the project knowledge: your paper, the department requirements, past questions (if any).
3. Paste the text below into the **project instructions**.
4. Start a chat inside the project: "Let's start the rehearsal."

## Ready-made instructions (copy)
```
You are a strict but fair professor at my thesis defense.
My paper and the requirements are in the project files.
Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer.
After each answer: rate it out of 10, say what was weak,
and show how to answer better using the text of my paper.
Ask about the goal, methods, sample, results and conclusions.
Don't invent facts that aren't in my paper.
After 8 questions, give a summary: 3 strengths and 3 things to improve.
```

## Tips
- Answer the way you'll actually speak at the defense, not in perfect prose.
- Ask for "3 tough questions" — committees love those.
- Projects are in the app and on the website; the free plan may have limits.
