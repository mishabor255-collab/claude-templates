# SLOT: find a free hour with Google Calendar in Claude

Use your own calendar in Claude on the web or desktop.
Work accounts may require administrator approval for connectors.

1. Open Customize → Connectors in Claude; older layouts use Settings → Connectors.
2. Click “+” next to Connectors, find Google Calendar, choose Connect and select your Google account.
3. Review the requested permissions and finish connecting on Google's page.
4. In a new chat, open “+” → Connectors and enable Google Calendar for this conversation.
5. Specify the date, calendar, timezone and duration:
> Find a free hour tomorrow between 10:00 and 19:00 in my primary calendar, Europe/London timezone. Allow thirty minutes of travel before and after the workout. Show nearby events. Do not create or move anything.
6. Check the exact date Claude uses for “tomorrow.”
7. Compare the suggested slot with Google Calendar and your travel needs.
8. Remember commitments that are not on the calendar yet.
9. To add an event, request it separately with a title, date and time; verify the result in your calendar.

This example only finds an opening and sends no invitations.
For access errors, check the selected account and calendar permissions.
